Project focus

retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over internal documents, and automated workflows
tightly integrated with core business systems. The stack is TypeScript and Next.js end to
end, and this is applied engineering on systems already in production, not model training
or fine-tuning.

- Proven track record taking over and improving existing codebases under live production traffic
- Practical retrieval expertise: hybrid search, embedding model selection, cross-encoder reranking, metadata filtering, permission-aware retrieval, structured diagnosis of poor retrieval quality

- Familiarity with Postgres and vector search (pgvector or comparable), Docker, Git, CI/CD, and a major cloud platform
- Hands-on experience with the Anthropic and/or OpenAI TypeScript SDKs
- Confident English communication; German is a plus
